Using Graph AI to Detect Wholesale CBDC Liquidity Bottlenecks

This article explores how central banks can utilize Graph Anomaly Artificial Intelligence (AI) to identify and prevent systemic liquidity bottlenecks within wholesale Central Bank Digital Currency (wCBDC) settlement networks. As financial institutions move toward instantaneous digital settlements, traditional monitoring tools struggle to keep pace with complex transaction webs. By mapping banks as nodes and transactions as edges, Graph AI models can analyze network topologies in real time. This allows central banks to detect abnormal liquidity hoarding, predict cascading settlement failures, and intervene with automated monetary tools before a localized bottleneck turns into a broader financial crisis.

The Challenge of Wholesale CBDC Liquidity

Wholesale CBDCs are designed to modernize interbank settlements, making high-value transactions between commercial banks and the central bank faster and more secure. Unlike traditional batch processing systems, wCBDC networks operate continuously in real time.

While this instantaneous nature improves efficiency, it also introduces new liquidity risks. In a real-time gross settlement environment, banks rely heavily on incoming payments to fund their outgoing obligations. If one major institution experiences a technical failure or decides to hoard liquidity due to market stress, it creates a bottleneck. Because the network is tightly interconnected, this single delay can rapidly cascade, freezing liquidity across the entire financial system before human regulators can react.

How Graph Anomaly AI Works in Settlement Networks

Traditional financial monitoring looks at data in flat tables, evaluating individual transactions or isolated bank balance sheets. This approach misses the broader picture of how money flows through the system. Graph Anomaly AI solves this by treating the wCBDC ecosystem as a dynamic, interconnected web.

In a graph representation of a wCBDC network:

  • Nodes represent the participating commercial banks and financial institutions.
  • Edges represent the payment channels and transactions flowing between these institutions.
  • Weights on the edges represent the volume, value, and velocity of the funds being transferred.

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) continuously process this structure to learn the "normal" behavior of the settlement network. The AI establishes a baseline for how liquidity typically circulates at any given millisecond, accounting for standard market fluctuations, time-of-day volume shifts, and seasonal trends.

Detecting Systemic Bottlenecks in Real Time

Once the Graph AI understands normal network topology, it monitors the live wCBDC ledger for structural anomalies that signal a developing liquidity trap. The AI detects bottlenecks through three primary mechanisms:

1. Identifying "Liquidity Sinks"

A liquidity sink occurs when a node (a bank) continues to receive incoming payments (edges) from the network but abruptly stops sending outgoing payments. Graph AI flags this asymmetrical flow immediately. It distinguishes between a bank simply having a quiet trading hour and a bank actively absorbing the market's liquidity without recycling it.

2. Spotting Cyclical Deadlocks

In complex interbank markets, Bank A might be waiting for funds from Bank B, which is waiting on Bank C, which is waiting on Bank A. Traditional monitoring sees three separate banks pausing transactions. Graph AI sees a closed loop—a cyclical deadlock. By identifying these rings of trapped obligations instantly, the AI highlights exactly where the gridlock is occurring.

3. Predicting Contagion Paths

When a bottleneck begins to form at a specific node, Graph Anomaly AI runs rapid simulations to predict the blast radius. By analyzing the strength and dependency of the edges connected to the failing node, the AI calculates which downstream banks will run out of liquidity next, giving central banks a clear timeline of the impending contagion.

Automated Central Bank Intervention

Detecting a bottleneck is only useful if the central bank can act on the information fast enough to stop a crisis. Integrating Graph AI directly into the wCBDC infrastructure allows for automated or semi-automated regulatory interventions.

When the AI detects a cyclical deadlock, it can instantly trigger a Liquidity-Saving Mechanism (LSM). The AI calculates the minimum amount of capital required to break the deadlock and instructs the wCBDC smart contracts to net out the offsetting payments simultaneously.

If the AI identifies a severe liquidity sink caused by market panic, it can alert the central bank's monetary policy desk to inject targeted emergency liquidity directly into the affected nodes. By surgically providing funds to the exact point of friction in the graph, the central bank prevents a systemic freeze without having to flood the entire market with excess capital.
